Numeraire Account gives you a wallet on Numeraire’s hosted SwissKnife instance. Your wallet records your balance and transactions for a particular asset and network. You can have several wallets under the same login.

Numeraire operates the ledger and the node that settles payments. An authorized administrator can create an invoice or send a payment on behalf of your wallet. To operate the ledger and node yourself, follow the [SwissKnife deployment guide](../swissknife/deployment/index.mdx).

You can get started by [registering a Numeraire account](./register.mdx).

<WalletOverview />

### What can I do with a Numeraire Account?

- Access to a powerful and user-friendly dashboard
- Own one or more asset-scoped wallets
- Send and receive Bitcoin at the speed of light
- Register a LN address (*your_username@numeraire.tech*)
- Send and receive Zaps on Nostr
- Sort and filter your transactions
- Manage a list of contacts
- Generate invoices
- Programmatically access your account and wallets with API Keys
- And much more...
